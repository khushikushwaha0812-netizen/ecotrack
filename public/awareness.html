♻️ Waste Awareness

🥬 Wet Waste (Green bin)

Food waste, vegetable waste, fruit peels, organic waste. Compost it if possible.

📦 Dry Waste (Blue bin)

Paper, cardboard, plastic, metal, glass. Keep it clean and dry.

🔌 E-Waste

Phones, chargers, batteries, electronic devices. Never throw in normal bins. Request a pickup.

✅ DO

Separate waste at home.
Use dustbins.
Reuse and recycle.
Report overflowing bins.

❌ DON'T

Don't mix wet and dry waste.
Don't burn waste.
Don't dump waste on roads.
Don't throw batteries in bins.
